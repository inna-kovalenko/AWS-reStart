# AI App Analysis: CV Summary Tailoring Tool

## Who is the customer?

Job seekers who are actively applying to multiple roles, especially career switchers, newcomers to a job market, or people targeting positions across different industries or specializations. The typical user is someone with solid experience but struggling to present it in a way that matches each specific job posting.

## Who is affected?

The applicant is the direct user. They gain time, reduce stress, and improve their chances. Indirectly, recruiters benefit too because they receive CVs that are easier to evaluate and more clearly relevant to the role they posted.

## What is the customer problem or opportunity?

Every job posting is slightly different. Rewriting a CV summary for each one feels tedious, but sending a generic summary reduces the chances of passing an applicant tracking system (ATS) or catching a recruiter's eye. Most applicants either send the exact same CV everywhere, which is risky, or spend significant time manually tweaking the wording for each application, which is exhausting and hard to scale. The opportunity is to close that gap by making a personalized, job-specific CV summary fast and easy to produce.

## What are you proposing to launch?

A text generator app. The user provides two inputs: their existing CV summary and the job description they are targeting. The app rewrites the summary to align with the job's language, keywords, and priorities, while preserving the applicant's real background and voice.

## Why does the customer need this in their life?

Recruiters typically spend less than ten seconds on an initial CV review. If the summary does not reflect the language and priorities of the job post, the applicant risks being overlooked even when well qualified. This is especially true for career switchers or applicants moving between countries or industries, where framing experience in the right words matters as much as the experience itself.

## How does it work?

The user pastes their current CV summary into one input field and the job description into another. The AI reads both, identifies the key skills, terms, and priorities from the job posting, and rewrites the CV summary to highlight the most relevant parts of the applicant's background using the employer's own language. The output is a ready-to-use summary of three to five sentences that the applicant can copy directly or adjust before submitting.
